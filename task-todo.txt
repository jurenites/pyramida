2. Delivery & Storage Bug Fixes
Storage Cascade Bug Fix
Problem: Citizens stall/freeze when their primary target pile fills up.
Specification: When a citizen arrives at a full pile, trigger a re-pathing check:
Search for the nearest alternative storage/warehouse that accepts the carried item and has space.
If found, re-route the citizen.
If no valid storage exists, transition the citizen to a DropOnGround state or Idle / Blocked state.
Delivery Interaction & Visual Polish
Labor Timer without UI Bar: Delivering resources to a storage pile takes 1.0 second of labor, but the progress bar UI overhead is hidden to prevent visual clutter.
Manual Storage Redirect: If the player manually orders a citizen who is carrying a resource directly to a storage site, immediately cancel their current task and assign the higher-priority task: Deliver Carried Item to Target Storage.
Detailed Case RulesCase 1 (Matches Required Material):Condition: Citizen carries a Log; Construction Site needs Logs (e.g., 1/4).Action: Citizen walks directly to site -> Deposits Log (Site becomes 2/4) -> Automatically begins applying construction labor (3 seconds per step).Case 2 (Mismatched Material, Target Material Stored):Condition: Citizen carries Berries; Construction Site needs Logs (1/4); Logs exist in nearby storage.Action: Show speech bubble feedback -> Citizen carries Berries to Storage -> Drops Berries -> Picks up Log from Storage -> Delivers Log to Construction Site -> Performs construction labor.Case 3 (Mismatched Material, Target Material Loose/Not in Storage):Condition: Citizen carries Berries; Construction Site needs Logs; Storage has no Logs (but loose Logs exist on the map).Action: Citizen drops Berries on the ground immediately (0 labor required to drop or pick up loose ground items) -> Walks to nearby loose Log -> Picks up Log -> Delivers to site & constructs.Case 4 (Materials Fully Delivered, Only Labor Needed):Condition: Site materials are satisfied (e.g., Logs 4/4), but construction labor is pending.Action: Citizen drops carried items on the ground instantly -> Proceeds directly to construction site -> Performs construction labor loop until build complete.
